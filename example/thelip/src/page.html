thelip knows 51 words in one sentence shape: command · colour · preposition · letter · digit · adverb. It reads those and nothing else.
What this is. thelip is The Lip, the lip reader from the syrup library, running in JavaScript in your browser, on the phone. Nothing leaves the device and no audio is used. Its network is LipNet (Assael, Shillingford, Whiteson, de Freitas 2016; weights from rizkiarm/LipNet, MIT), trained on the GRID corpus: 64 of 66 words on GRID's sample clips. A new face and a phone camera are harder than the lab data it learned from, so expect misses, and say the sentence at a normal pace.
Live. With the camera, an utterance is read as soon as your lips go still; on a fast phone the words form while you speak. From a recording, the clip plays at the speed thelip reads it, with the subtitle forming underneath.
